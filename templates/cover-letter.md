# Cover Letter Template

A short, honest cover letter. Three or four paragraphs, half a page. Fill the
`{{...}}` placeholders, delete this heading and the notes in _italics_, then
export. Same rules as the resume: real claims only, plain human voice, no
buzzwords (leverage, spanning, robust, seamless, delve), no em dashes as
sentence breaks.

Dear {{Hiring Manager name / Hiring Team}},

_Opening (2–3 sentences): name the role and where you saw it, then one sentence
on why this company/team specifically. Say something concrete about them so it
does not read as a form letter._

I am applying for the {{role}} role at {{Company}}. {{One specific, genuine
reason you want this team — a product, a data problem, a domain you know.}}

_Body 1 (fit): pick the two or three requirements from the posting you match
best and back each with one real thing you have done. Mirror the posting's
wording where it is honestly true._

In my most recent role at {{Company}}, I {{concrete achievement tied to a top
requirement in the posting}}. {{Second achievement tied to another
requirement.}} {{Optional third.}}

_Body 2 (why you, briefly): one differentiator — a project, a tuning win, a
domain crossover — that a bullet list would not convey._

{{One paragraph on a project or strength that shows depth, e.g. a portfolio
build or a performance/cost improvement.}}

_Close: thank them, state availability/interest, invite next step. One or two
sentences._

I would welcome the chance to talk about how I can help {{Company}}'s data team.
Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
{{Your Name}}
